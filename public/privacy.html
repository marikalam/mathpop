MathPop Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

MathPop is a math practice app made by Marika Lam. It collects nothing: no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This policy covers the MathPop iPhone app and the website at marikalam.github.io/apps/mathpop.

What MathPop saves

MathPop remembers a few things so it picks up where you left off: the grade you chose, your answer settings, and how many problems were answered and how many were right. All of it stays on your device. None of it is sent to us or anyone else, and there are no accounts to sign in to.

Handwriting

When you write an answer with your finger, the numbers are read by a small model that runs entirely on your device. Your drawing is never uploaded or saved.

Speech and sounds

The voice that reads problems aloud is generated on your device. In the iPhone app, everything it needs is built in, and the Explore Numbers voices in other languages use the speech voices already on your iPhone. On the website, the voice files are downloaded the first time from public file hosts (Hugging Face and jsDelivr), which, like any website, can see your IP address when your browser downloads from them. Nothing you type or write is sent to them.

Children

MathPop is made for kids to use, ideally with a parent or teacher nearby. It doesn't ask for any personal information, and kids don't need to enter any to play.

Deleting your data

Everything MathPop saves is on your device. Delete the MathPop app, or clear the website's data in your browser settings, and it's gone.
