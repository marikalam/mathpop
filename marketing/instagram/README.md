# MathPop on Instagram (@playmathpop)

Everything needed to launch the account: profile picture, ten launch posts in
`posts/` (1080×1350, Instagram's 4:5 feed size), captions and a four-week plan.
Rebuild the images after changing the app screenshots with
`node marketing/instagram/build.mjs` (needs `npm install` first).

## 1. Create the accounts (about 10 minutes, on your phone)

**Gmail: playmathpop@gmail.com**
1. Gmail app → your profile picture → *Add another account* → Google → *Create account* → *For my personal use*.
2. First name `MathPop`, leave last name blank. Choose *Create your own Gmail address* → `playmathpop`.
3. Verify with your phone number and add marikaleelam@gmail.com as the recovery email.

**Instagram: @playmathpop**
1. Instagram app → *Create new account* → *Sign up with email* → playmathpop@gmail.com.
2. Name `MathPop`, username `playmathpop`.
3. Settings → *Account type and tools* → *Switch to professional account* → **Creator** → category **Education**.
4. Profile picture: `profile-picture.png`.
5. Edit profile → paste the bio and link below.
6. Turn on two-factor authentication (Settings → Accounts Center → Password and security).

## 4. First four weeks

Post 3 times a week (Mon, Wed, Sat mornings) and add 3 to 4 stories a week.
Reels reach far more people than photos, so aim for at least one a week.

| Week | Mon | Wed | Sat | Stories |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Launch grid (posts 01–09) | Reel: 15-second screen recording of one full round, ending on the sticker | Post 10 (Math you can see) | Poll: "What grade is your kid in?" · Behind the scenes of building it |
| 2 | Puzzle post (new problem; make it with the same template) | Reel: your kid's hands playing (no face needed), with the app voice as audio | Answer + trick post | Quiz sticker with a math problem · Share the sticker book |
| 3 | Carousel: "5 ways to practice math at the grocery store" | Reel: "Show me how" walkthrough, recorded on screen | Languages: the same number in 4 languages | Ask: "What math topic is hardest at your house?" |
| 4 | Answer the most common reply from week 3 | Reel: Pre-K counting with ladybugs | Feature request round-up / "what's new in MathPop" | Countdown to App Store launch |

## 5. Growing it

- **15 minutes a day** of commenting (not just liking) on posts in #homeschoolmath, #singaporemath and kindergarten-teacher accounts gets more early followers than anything else.
- Reply to every comment within the first hour after posting.
- Send DMs to 10 small homeschool and mom accounts (2k–20k followers) offering MathPop free in exchange for an honest story. Small accounts reply far more often than big ones.
- Share each post to your personal stories and family group chats in week 1. The first 50 followers are the hardest.
- After 4 weeks, check *Professional dashboard* → which posts got the most saves and shares, then make more like those.
