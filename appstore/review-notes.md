MathPop is a math practice game for Kindergarten through 4th grade, made for
the Kids category (ages 6–8). No account or sign-in is needed.

How to use it:
- Choose a grade with the "Grade" button at the top of the home page. The
  six Practice cards change to fit that grade (for example Kindergarten:
  Addition, Subtraction, Comparing, Make a Ten, Place Value, Word Problems;
  3rd grade adds Multiplication and Stack & Solve).
- Tap any card to start a round of ten problems. Answer by tapping a choice,
  typing, or writing the number with a finger (⚙️ Settings on the home page
  switches between these). Tap the problem to hear it read aloud.
- Random Mix mixes the grade's operations; Explore Numbers lets the child tap
  0–10 and hear each number in English, Japanese, Cantonese or Mandarin.

Kids category notes:
- No links out of the app, no purchases, no ads, no analytics, no tracking,
  and no third-party services. The app makes no network requests.
- Nothing is collected. Progress (problems solved and how many were right)
  and settings are stored only on the device.
- The read-aloud voice is the open-source Piper text-to-speech model bundled
  inside the app; Explore Numbers' other languages use the iPhone's built-in
  voices. Handwriting is read by a small on-device model; drawings are never
  saved or sent anywhere.

The interface is in English and works the same in all regions.
